SAP Innovation Awards 2025 - Sustainability Hero
HARTING Technology Group
Electronic Connectivity Components · Espelkamp, Germany
HARTING, an early sustainability pioneer, faced significant challenges with inefficient and time-consuming CO₂ footprint calculations across its global manufacturing operations. To scale and automate emissions tracking, HARTING implemented SAP Sustainability Footprint Management - achieving automated, data-driven GHG calculations that replaced manual spreadsheet processes and enabled transparent reporting across the entire product portfolio.
